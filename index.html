Initiatives

What we do.

Education

Seminars covering probability, statistics, systematic strategies, market microstructure, and related topics.

Trading games and competitions

Market-making games, trading simulations, and poker nights. Members also team up for intercollegiate trading competitions.

Research and engineering

Members build trading platforms, market simulators, and backtesting tools in quantitative research projects using market data.

Careers and networking

Resume reviews, mock interviews, and recruiting advice from members who have interned at top trading firms, as well as mentorship from industry professionals.
